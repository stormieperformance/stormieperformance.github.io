What Stormiē is

Stormiē is a strength decision system for people who train more than one thing (gym + sport). It helps you avoid doing too much, too little, or the wrong work.

It helps you decide

  • How many gym sessions to do this week
  • How hard to train
  • What to focus on right now

Developed by Storm Kolmodin. Licensed Personal Trainer since 2018.
