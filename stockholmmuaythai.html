One gym. One system.

Stockholm Muay Thai has strong identity, culture and credibility. The digital experience is fragmented across external systems.

This concept brings everything into one branded flow — from first visit to membership to merch.

Ecosystem

Branded Website

The club home. Schedule, coaches, culture and entry point into the gym.

Membership System

Cleaner member-facing flow for plans and onboarding. Backend systems can remain, but the experience is controlled.

Club Merch

Profile products and apparel presented under the club identity, instead of a generic external store.

Beginner Onboarding

A clear starting point for new members. From curiosity to first session to full membership.
