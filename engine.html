How it works
The integration problem between strength and sport.
After analysis, the engine generates
  • Strength frequency (sessions/week)
  • Intensity limit (RPE cap)
  • Back-off volume adjustment
  • Structural focus bias

Definitions

Overreach Index = heuristic risk metric based on sport stress + effort + fatigue state.

Intensity limit = maximum effort allowed under current integration stress (RPE cap).

Back-off volume = volume after your top set (scaled down when stress is high).

Performance protection = prioritize sport output when fatigue risk is elevated.

RPE = effort rating (10 = max, 8 ≈ ~2 reps in reserve).
