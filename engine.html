Glossary
Effort cap

Maximum effort allowed for working sets (expressed as an RPE limit). Keeps progress without draining recovery.

Intensity limit

Upper limit on how heavy you go relative to current capacity. Used when sport load is high.

Back-off volume

Additional sets done after a top set at lighter load. Scales down when sport load is high.

Protect output

Prioritize performance in your sport by limiting strength stress when fatigue risk is high.

Recovery bias

The engine shifts your week toward recovery when total training stress is high.

Focus area

Which qualities the prescription leans toward (durability, max strength, pulling capacity, etc.).
