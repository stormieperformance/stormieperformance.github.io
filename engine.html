How it works
The integration problem between strength and sport.
After analysis, the engine generates
  • Strength frequency (sessions/week)
  • Intensity limit (RPE cap)
  • Back-off volume adjustment
  • Structural focus bias
  • Performance protection directive

Definitions

Intensity limit = maximum effort allowed under current sport load.

Back-off volume = volume after your top set (scaled down when load is high).

Performance protection = prioritize performance recovery when fatigue risk is high.

Structural focus bias = what quality the plan prioritizes this week.

RPE = effort rating (10 = max, 8 ≈ 2 reps in reserve).
