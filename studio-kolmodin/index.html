ABOUT THE STUDIO

A private studio for firms where reputation is the asset.

Studio Kolmodin works with a deliberately small number of investment managers, family offices and advisory firms. Every engagement is led personally, from the first conversation to long-term care of the finished site.

We take on engagements where:

  • ◆Credibility with investors and partners is at stake
  • ◆The current site no longer reflects the firm
  • ◆Several audiences need clear, separate paths
  • ◆Discretion matters as much as design
